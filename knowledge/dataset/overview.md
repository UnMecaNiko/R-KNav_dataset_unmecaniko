These notes were checked on: 2026-09-06.

## What it is

A **sidewalk navigation** dataset from Robot.com's (formerly Kiwibot) **R-Kiwi** fleet. Collected across ~15 US campuses/cities during real deliveries. Intended for research on navigation **vision-language-action (VLA)** models, not for a classic LiDAR stack.

Figures published by Robot.com (LinkedIn / Hub card, 2026; verify before citing in a paper): 400+ rovers in the fleet, 250,000+ autonomous miles, 300,000+ deliveries. Announced total corpus: **10,000 hours**. The Hub does not host those 10k h.

Card authors: Yaisa Catalina Ramirez Cepeda, Pedro Alejandro Gonzalez, John A. Betancourt (Beta). Contact for the 10k h: `airobotics@kiwicampus.com`.

Format: **LeRobot v3.0** (`codebase_version: v3.0` in the sample). `robot_type: rover`.

## Robot and sensors (as published)

Differential drive. Four RGB cameras (main, left, right, rear), GPS/RTK, IMU. **3D LiDAR deliberately excluded** (vision emphasis). Actions: `linear.x` and `angular.z` (velocity commands). Odometry state: the same pair, measured.

Mission routes: **OSRM** over enriched OSM. ROS 2 capture (rosbags) → trimming into episodes (motion, GPS quality, maneuvers) → MCAP + YAML → LeRobot with an adapted **Rosetta** ([iblnkn/rosetta](https://github.com/iblnkn/rosetta)). *As-of-nearest* synchronization against the front camera. Anonymization: faces and license plates.

Driving modes: **autonomous (custom Nav2) and teleoperation**. The Hub does **not** ship a per-frame `teleop` / `autonomous` field. `action` is whoever had the wheel. Sentences like *Turn left at the white van…* are generated by a **VLM after the fact**; they were not Nav2's instruction.

## FoMo

*Foundation Models*, not "fear of missing out". Train on R-KNav and request evaluation on real R-Kiwis. KPIs: interventions per mile and average speed vs their own stack. Threshold ~50% to pass the filter; biannual cutoffs. Prize: infrastructure and robots. Submissions: "coming soon" on robot.com (2026-08-23). The Hub form already asks about interest.

This lab does **not** assume FoMo enrollment. A small policy over the sample is not a serious submission.

## What it is not

It is not nuScenes nor a car dataset. It is not a ROS bag ready for `ros2 bag play` (topics have to be reconstructed). It does not include Nav2's internal plan, only the result (`action` / odom / GPS / video).
